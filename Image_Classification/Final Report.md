In part 1, the basic classifier was able to identify my Rottweiler in the image at 0.57, Appenzeller at 0.13, and Gordon setter at 0.09. Ading Grad-CAM was pretty easy with some AI help, and the heatmap glowed bright on the dog's body, head, and fur, with most of the background image left cold (blue). That shows me the model locks onto the dog in the image and focuses on things like the jaw and markings. This is probably why the confidence was so high when rating the rottweiler at 0.57.If the background was lit up more from the heatmap then it might have been thrown off when guessing the breed. Since it stayed focus on the dog, it was more accurate with its guessing.

In part 2, the basic blur made everything soft with the Gaussian filter after shrinking the size. This took away details from the dog's eyes and coat edges. My custom pop art filter increased the contrast, colors, and added inverted edges for outlines. It made the Rottweiler more bold and vibrant in the image. I tested it on a landscape picture as well to make sure it didn't wash out any colors. This setup allows for AI to make quick dramatic changes while a human can fine tune it for a more refined look afterwards.
